### Benchmark: Blinkfire 2025-26 NBA Report (vendor; team and league accounts, not creators)
Source: https://www.blinkfire.com/d/view_upload/2025-26_nba_season_report (PDF, read 2026-11-03). Covers 2025-10-21 to 2026-04-12 regular season. Methodology p.3: official owned/earned accounts, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, X, Threads.
- Format mix, Instagram (p.12, last 4 seasons): images 40%/37%/31%/29%, video 53%/53%/48%/51%, mixed media 7%/10%/21%/20% (2022-23 to 2025-26). Video plus mixed = 71% of 2025-26 posts.
- TikTok post volume (p.11): league account 1,541 (2022-23) to 15,548 (2025-26); 30 teams 3,602 to 12,307. "Surged 10x in four seasons."
- TikTok league vs teams (p.10): league 154.4M engagements vs 131.2M for all 30 teams combined (1.18x).
- TikTok per-post (p.13): 421K average views per post, 9.4% engagement rate. Highest per-post efficiency of platforms in report.
- Instagram (p.4, p.9, p.13): engagements -35% YoY, but shares +27%. Instagram accounts = 73% of league views. Instagram 192M team followers.
- Engagement-rate leaders (p.8): TikTok Phoenix Suns 10.9; Instagram Minnesota 7.5. Most-followed teams rarely lead engagement rate.
- Follower growth (p.5-6): Instagram/FB hold 54% of team followers (p.7). TikTok +20.8% San Antonio (Wembanyama content). X: every team declined.
- Legal note: the report's sample posts are league and team clips with logos (NBA footage). Not usable as a format template for us without licence.

## Patterns

- Video-first is now the norm for league-level stat-adjacent accounts (Blinkfire p.12): about 7 in 10 Instagram posts are video or mixed media, and images have fallen from 40% to 29% in four seasons.
- Shares beat likes as the growth signal in a down year (Blinkfire p.4, p.9): a shareable frame is the thing to design for.

## Synthesis so far (iteration 10)

Caveat first: almost all sourced evidence is league, team or media-brand level (NBA, Blinkfire, House of Highlights, Front Office Sports), plus 2017-2020 industry studies. Independent stats-niche creator profiles with views and posting data are not yet found. Confidence is set accordingly.

- **Posts per day (estimate, medium-low):** a team TikTok account posts about 2.4 per day (Blinkfire 2025-26: 12,307 team posts / 30 / ~174 days). Instagram posting cadence not sourced. House of Highlights and NBA describe "night in and night out" coverage, not a number. Source: https://www.blinkfire.com/d/view_upload/2025-26_nba_season_report (read 2026-11-03).
- **Video vs image (high for direction, medium for size):** Instagram NBA-team mix 2025-26 is video 51%, mixed media 20%, images 29%. Images fell from 40% in 2022-23. Supported by Blinkfire p.12 (one vendor source, four seasons).
- **Post-type engagement (low; old):** Socialinsider 22M posts, 2017-20: mixed carousel 2.33%, carousel 1.92%, image 1.74%, video 1.45%.
