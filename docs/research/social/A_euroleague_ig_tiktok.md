### A1. Official league accounts

- **@euroleague (men's), Instagram.** About 1.34M followers at the end of the 2024-25 regular season, up 22%. Instagram was the top platform for the league. Source: EuroLeague press release, [euroleaguebasketball.net](https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/news/euroleague-social-media-reaches-new-heights-with-record-numbers), and [eurohoops.net 2025 Final Four digital release](https://www.eurohoops.net/en/euroleague/1842974/euroleague-basketball-achieves-record-breaking-digital-and-social-media-success-during-2025-final-four/amp/). ## Patterns

Each pattern below has at least two independent sources, unless marked single-source.

- **Video is the growth engine.** League video views rose from 1.49B (2025-26 regular season, [Eurohoops, 2026-05-08](https://www.eurohoops.net/en/euroleague/1965635/2025-26-euroleague-regular-season-breaks-all-historic-records/)) and to about 300M total at the 2026 Final Four ([Eurohoops, 2026-07-15](https://www.eurohoops.net/en/euroleague/1991297/2026-euroleague-final-four-athens-presented-by-etihad-sets-new-digital-benchmarks/amp/)). Conflict: a second Eurohoops summary gives 2.27B (+49%), undated. Two sources, so medium confidence on direction, low on the exact number.
- **Live shows on TikTok.** The league streams game-day shows on TikTok ([LiveU case](https://www.liveu.tv/?p=438928), 2025-26) and the 2025 Final Four semifinals and final ([EuroLeague](https://www.euroleaguebasketball.net/euroleague/news/in-a-first-for-any-team-or-ball-sport-euroleague-streams-final-four-on-tiktok), read via search snippet only, page 429). Format: pre- and post-buzzer streams, arena interviews, live chat Q&A.
- **Creators as a channel.** Amplifier had 17 named creators in 2025 (mostly players and personalities) and 35+ creators in 2026, with creator-led views (146M) about equal to owned-channel views (145.6M) at the 2026 Final Four. Sources: Eurohoops 2025-05-21 and 2026-07-15. Medium confidence.
- **Club views are led by Greek and Serbian clubs.** Blinkfire H2 2024 views: Panathinaikos 90M and Olympiacos 55.2M, behind Partizan 84.1M and Crvena Zvezda 73.5M ([Eurohoops, 2025-04-23](https://eurohoops.net/en/euroleague/1815370/euroleague-clarifies-reports-on-clubs-social-media-presence)). League says a leaked version had inaccuracies, so use this as the official-tool figure with caution.
- **TikTok is smaller than Instagram for the league.** @euroleague TikTok 79K (undated) versus IG ~1.34M (2024-25). Single source for TikTok count, so low confidence.
- **Trend-and-player content.** House of Marketers' campaign used players, coaches and mascots and TikTok trends ([case study](https://houseofmarketers.com/euroleague-basketball-tiktok-influencer-case-study/), 2023). Brandwatch says clubs share highlights, BTS and player features. Two sources, medium.
- **Greek niche pages show high engagement at small size.** panathinaikos24.gr 23.5K IG at 6.80% ER versus gazzetta.gr 191.9K at 5.09% (Starngage snippet, 2026). Single source, low confidence.

No source gave: posting cadence per EuroLeague account, hook wording, caption length, hashtag sets, best posting times, or animation/data-graphic style. These are the main open gaps.

## Synthesis at 30 (final, read 2026-11-03)

Caveat: 30 counted iterations (zero-gain iterations excluded per orchestrator), but the evidence is mostly press releases and third-party snippets. Posting cadence, hook and caption data are still not sourced for any EuroLeague or Greek basketball account.

**Baseline**

- **Posts per day/week:** not sourced for any EuroLeague, club or Greek basketball page. Benchmarks only: TikTok posting frequency rose 40% across account sizes (Social Insider 2026); fxbaogao 2023 median 1.9 videos/week. Confidence: low.
- **Video vs photo vs carousel:** video dominates reach (1.49B to 2.27B league video views, Eurohoops 2026). No format split was found. Confidence: medium on video, none on carousel share.
- **Topics:** game-day highlights, player and personality features, BTS, Final Four coverage, live shows. Confidence: medium (league and campaign sources agree).
- **Voice:** "impactful, fun and educational" (campaign brief, one source). Greek-language voice: not sourced. Confidence: low.
- **Animation and graphics:** no source found. Not measured.
- **Hook patterns:** no source. Not measured.
- **Caption length, hashtags, best times:** no source. Not measured.
- **Engagement benchmarks:** Rival IQ IG sports teams median 1.57%; Rival IQ TikTok sports teams 11% ER by view; Social Insider 2026 TikTok ER 4.20%. Third-party EL IG ER conflicts: HypeAuditor 3.49%, SpeakRJ 1.40% (2022). Confidence: medium on ranges, low on any one figure.
- **Copyright:** league and clubs own their footage and logos. The Amplifier program gives creators league access. Independent pages would carry the same legal risk when they reuse league footage.

**What this means for a new EuroLeague data page**

- **Market gap, stated with limits:** three search rounds (English, Greek, TikTok) found no independent EuroLeague stats or data-graphics account on IG or TikTok with public numbers. The strongest evidence is the search results repeating the same league and tracker pages. This is a gap in what search returns, not proof there is none. Confidence: medium. Verify by checking the handles directly before building a plan on it.
- **Scale expectation:** the league's own IG is about 1.34M and its TikTok about 79K. A new data page starts far below these, so the realistic peer is a niche Greek page (panathinaikos24.gr, 23.5K) with high ER, not the league.
- **Format to copy:** a fixed weekly data card (one stat, one chart, one line of voice) fits the league's live-show and creator model without footage. This is a design recommendation, not sourced evidence.
- **Measure, do not assume:** posting cadence, hooks and formats must be collected from the live handles over 2-4 weeks, since no source supplies them.

**Conflicts to resolve before use**

- House of Marketers campaign followers: 238,000 (case study read 2026-11-03) vs 168,000 (two Greek and English snippets).
- Video views: 1.49B (regular season, May 2026) vs 2.27B (undated Eurohoops summary).
- EuroLeague IG followers: 1.34M (league, 2024-25), 1.47M and 1.5M (HypeAuditor), 1.1M (Socialveins, snapshot).

## Five accounts most worth copying as a format (ranked, with the caveat that none was profiled at post level)

1. **panathinaikos24.gr (IG, 23.5K, 6.80% ER):** closest realistic peer, basketball-only news, high engagement at small size. Copy the niche focus. Post-level format not yet profiled.
2. **@euroleague TikTok live game-day shows (league, 79K TikTok):** a proven pattern of pre/post shows and live Q&A with a fixed host. Copy the show structure, not the footage.
3. **EuroLeague Amplifier creators (17-35+, league-sanctioned):** a creator-led model that matched owned views in 2026. Copy the creator-collab structure with a data angle.
4. **BasketNews_COM (YouTube, ~156K-194K subs, podcast 48 episodes):** a media brand that combines news, interviews and a podcast. Copy the long-form podcast to clip pipeline.
5. **Rival IQ / Social Insider benchmark practice:** not an account, but the only sourced sports ER ranges. Use these to set the target, since no EuroLeague account has a sourced ER.

Honest gap: none of these five was profiled at post level (posts per week, format mix, hooks, captions). Their ranking rests on follower counts, engagement snapshots and channel structure. Treat the ranking as a shortlist to profile next, not a finding.
