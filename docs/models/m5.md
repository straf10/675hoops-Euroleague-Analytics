# M5: roster-aware game predictor

Status: live shadow. The EuroLeague M5 is logged daily in `predictions/euroleague_m5_2026-27.csv` and scored in the live scorecard. On the Forecasts page it appears only in the round comparison beside Elo (`web/src/components/ForecastRecord.astro`, `FORECAST_MODELS` in `api/readmodel.py`). Elo stays the published forecast.

## What it is
M5 forecasts a game from the players expected to play. Team strength is the sum of each player's projected share of the minutes times his M3 rating (O + D per 100 possessions), scaled by the expected possessions. On top of that come a team residual for what the players do not explain (coaching, system, fit), home court, and rest as a declared option. The output is P(home win), an expected margin and an expected total, each with a predictive distribution. Over/under and spread-cover probabilities for any line follow from those distributions (`models/m5.py`: `p_over`, `p_cover`).

**Data:**
- EuroLeague: player ratings are M3's `rapm_spm` walk-forward ratings at the committed M3 parameters, not re-tuned. Warm-up 2011–2014 (stints start in 2011). Tuning 2015–2022, validation 2023, test 2024–2025.
- The backtest reads no live season. Its inputs stop at 2025. The live log appends the live season to the frame after the fact.

**Rest features** (`models/rest.py`), from EuroLeague tip-off times:
- days since the previous game (capped at 7);
- short rest (≤ 2.5 days);
- games in the last 7 days;
- whether the previous game was in the other competition.

The back-to-back flag (≤ 1.5 days) was replaced by short rest, because no game in either competition comes that soon after the previous one.

**Projected minutes** (`models/rotation.py`):
- `proj_hc`: M3's share over the team's previous 5 games;
- `proj_decay`: all earlier games of the season, exponentially weighted (half-life 1–8 games);
- `proj_avail`: `proj_decay`, dropping a player absent from the team's last 2 games.

There is no injury feed, so this is the only availability signal. Oracle minutes (the game's own minutes) are reported as an upper bound only.

**Team residual and rest** (`models/m5.py`, `fit_residual`): at every round cutoff, a time-decayed ridge on earlier games fits actual margin − player part = h + r_home − r_away (+ β · rest difference).

**Forms:**
- `core`: no rest;
- `core_rest`: with rest;
- `blend`: the `core_rest` margin, M1 and Elo with non-negative weights, fit each season on earlier seasons only.

**Distribution:** Student-t on the margin, fit on tuning seasons as M1 does. Platt recalibration is used only if it lowers tuning log loss.

**Totals:** M1's expected total, or M1's total plus a rest term fit on earlier seasons, chosen by tuning CRPS.

**Choice:** lowest tuning log loss over all candidates (414). A difference under half a thousandth counts as a tie. Ties go to the simpler form, then the simpler shares rule, then the lower loss. M5 picks on tuning, so validation stays a clean test of the choice.

**Live** (`src/eurohoops/live_m5.py`, run by `eurohoops predict`): writes the M5 log from the projected roster. It needs the 2007+ games mart and the 2011+ stints mart. The daily workflow stages them from the raw cache (`ingest --from-season 2007`, `stints --mart`). A failure there only skips M5. Elo and M1 forecasts are identical with and without that history. Logging rule: the live M5 logs confirmed, unplayed games of every round whose first tip-off is within 48 h of the run (`predict.upcoming_games`, shared with the Elo log). A game's scored row is its latest one stamped before tip-off.

**Flagship promotion check:** `eurohoops promote-check --competition euroleague --look 1|2` runs a look and writes `reports/promotion_euroleague_2026_look<N>.json`. Look 1 needs round 19 complete; look 2 needs every scheduled game of the games mart played. Promotion needs the paired-bootstrap CI upper bound below 0 and a mean M5 − Elo log-loss difference of −0.01 or lower. The report also holds `sensitivity_round_clustered` (whole season-rounds resampled) and an ELOR reported-only comparison; neither enters the decision.

## Results
**Tuning** (2015–2022, 2236 games). M5 is `proj_hc` | `core` (residual half-life 180 days, ridge 40), with no Platt step and totals M1 + rest. Tuning log loss 0.599344, against M1's 0.60602 and Elo's 0.609009. Rest, blend and Platt did not help on tuning: `core_rest` 0.599985, `blend` 0.602856, Platt 0.600889. The decayed and availability minutes projections were within the tie tolerance of `proj_hc`, so the simpler rule won.

**Validation gate** (2023, 331 games): passed. M5 0.583765 vs M1 0.587714, difference −0.003948, 95% CI [−0.014946, 0.006291]. Elo validation log loss is 0.588509.

**Test** (2024–2025, 732 games): M5 0.623203 vs M1 0.623218, difference −0.000015, 95% CI [−0.009546, 0.009753]. A tie. The validation edge did not carry to test, the same pattern as M3, whose ratings M5 uses. Margin RMSE 11.697405 vs M1's 11.656481.

**Totals** carry over. M1 + rest beats Elo's totals baseline on validation (CRPS −1.093183) and on test (−0.914002, CI [−1.220227, −0.576823]).

**Segment:** Panathinaikos/Olympiacos games (test, 163 games): M5 0.583363 vs M1 0.592644.

**Injury feed value** (projected vs oracle minutes). Oracle log loss is 0.580253 on validation and 0.620074 on test. On tuning it is worse than projected (0.600601): blowouts hand garbage-time minutes to the bench, and actual minutes carry that outcome. The gap is concentrated in the 31 test games where one of a team's three biggest minute-takers was missing from the projection. There the margin RMSE is 0.843347 worse, CI [0.08003, 1.648136].

**Rest** (`reports/m5_rest.json`, residual on rest differences):
- EuroLeague: no feature is distinguishable from 0 in any split. The other-competition previous game gives 1.506112 points on test, 90% CI [−0.84813, 3.860354].

**Retune** (`reports/m5_retune.json`, rolling-origin folds 2015–2023, 2567 games, 12 settings over residual half-life, minutes projection and roster turnover): the one-SE rule selected the current setting, `proj_hc`, turnover off, half-life 180 days. The baseline stands: no new version. Pooled log loss 0.597679 for both. The best setting, `proj_avail` with half-life 1 game, is 0.597590, inside the baseline's one SE of 0.000479. Recomputed as declared (whole-season bootstrap, seed 20261011), the SE is 0.000454. No fold has a consistent winner.

- Validation: M5 0.474807 vs M1 0.429759, CI of the difference [0.010791, 0.083421]. M5 is worse.
- Test: M5 0.478959 vs M1 0.48355. Totals CRPS −0.206531 vs M1.

## Limitations
- There is no injury or lineup feed. Availability is only inferred from recent box scores.
- Travel distance is not modelled.
- Player ratings are M3's and inherit its weakness on test.
- The distribution is fit in-sample on tuning (as M1), so tuning log losses are slightly optimistic. Validation and test are not affected.
