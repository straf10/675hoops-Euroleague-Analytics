# M5: roster-aware game predictor

Weeks 14–16 (`docs/history/prompts/week-14-16.md`, progress log `reports/week14-16_progress.md`).
Reports:
- `reports/backtest_m5.json` (EuroLeague backtest and gate), with per-game predictions in
  `reports/backtest_m5_games.csv`;
- `reports/backtest_m5_gbl.json` (GBL, reported only);
- `reports/m5_rest.json` (rest beyond M5).

Every decimal below is in the Numbers table with its report path, checked by
`tests/test_model_card_m5.py`.

## What it does
Forecasts a game from the players expected to play. Team strength is the sum of each player's
projected share of the minutes times his M3 rating (O + D per 100 possessions), scaled by the
expected possessions. On top of that come a team residual for what the players do not explain
(coaching, system, fit), home court and, as a declared option, rest. The output is P(home win),
an expected margin and an expected total, each with a predictive distribution. Over/under and
spread-cover probabilities for any line follow from those distributions (`models/m5.py`
`p_over`, `p_cover`).

## Data
- EuroLeague:
  - player ratings: M3's `rapm_spm` walk-forward ratings with the committed M3 parameters, not
    re-tuned (warm-up 2011–2014, as stints start in 2011);
  - splits: tuning 2015–2022, validation 2023, test 2024–2025.
- GBL: box-score-only player ratings (M3's `box_only`; there is no GBL RAPM), with the same split
  seasons as M1 GBL.
- The live season is never read by the backtest: its inputs stop at 2025, so the daily log cannot move a report (the live log appends the live season to the frame, after the fact).
- Rest features (`models/rest.py`), from the tip-off times of both competitions:
  - days since the previous game (capped at 7);
  - short rest (≤ 2.5 days);
  - games in the last 7 days;
  - whether the previous game was in the other competition.

  Panathinaikos and Olympiacos link through `config.GREEK_EL_CLUBS`. The declared back-to-back
  flag (≤ 1.5 days) was replaced by short rest before any fit, because no game in either
  competition comes that soon after the previous one.

## Method
- **Projected minutes** (`models/rotation.py`), declared variants:
  - `proj_hc`: M3's share over the team's previous 5 games;
  - `proj_decay`: all earlier games of the season, exponentially weighted (half-life 1–8 games);
  - `proj_avail`: `proj_decay`, dropping a player absent from the team's last 2 games.

  There is no injury feed, so this is the only availability signal.
- **Oracle minutes:** the game's own minutes. Reported as an upper bound, never chosen, gated or
  logged.
- **Team residual and rest** (`models/m5.py` `fit_residual`): at every round cutoff, a
  time-decayed ridge on earlier games fits actual margin − player part = h + r_home − r_away
  (+ β·rest difference).
- **Forms:**
  - `core` (no rest);
  - `core_rest`;
  - `blend`: the `core_rest` margin, M1 and Elo with non-negative weights, fit each season on
    earlier seasons only.
- **Distribution:** Student-t on the margin, fit on the tuning seasons as M1 does. Platt
  recalibration was kept only if it lowered tuning log loss.
- **Totals:** M1's expected total, or M1's total plus a rest term fit on earlier seasons, chosen
  by tuning CRPS.
- **Choice:** lowest tuning log loss over all candidates. A difference under the declared tie
  tolerance (`M5Backtest.tie_tolerance`, half a thousandth) counts as a tie, and ties go to the
  simpler form, then the simpler shares rule, then the lower loss.

  PLAN §5.6 says "pick by log loss on validation"; M5 picks on tuning so that validation stays a
  clean test of the choice.

## Tuning and the choice
Of the declared candidates, M5 chose **`proj_hc` | `core`** (residual half-life 180 days, ridge
40), with no Platt step and totals **M1 + rest**. Its tuning log loss is 0.599344, against M1's
0.60602 and Elo's 0.609009, on the same 2,236 games.
- **Rest, blend and Platt:** none of them helped on tuning (`core_rest` 0.599985, `blend`
  0.602856, Platt 0.600889).
- **Minutes projection:** the decayed and availability projections were within the tie
  tolerance of `proj_hc`, so the simpler rule won.
- **Grid:** widened once before the verdict, because the first best values were on its lower
  edges. The choice was then made with the D10 tie rule.

**Result: PASS.** M5 0.583765 vs M1 0.587714 on 331 games, difference −0.003948, 95% CI

## Test (2024–2025, scored once after the validation commit)
M5 0.623203 vs M1 0.623218 on 732 games, difference −0.000015, 95% CI [−0.009546, 0.009753]: a
tie. **The validation edge did not carry to test**, the same pattern as M3, whose player ratings
M5 stands on. Margin RMSE is 11.697405 vs M1's 11.656481.

What does carry is **totals**. M1 + rest beats Elo's totals baseline on validation (CRPS
−1.093183) and on test (−0.914002, CI [−1.220227, −0.576823]).

Panathinaikos/Olympiacos games (test, 163 games) favour M5: 0.583363 vs 0.592644. This is a
reported segment, not a claim.

## Value of an injury feed (projected vs oracle minutes)
Actual minutes help little on average. Oracle log loss is 0.580253 on validation and 0.620074 on
test, and on tuning it is even worse than projected (0.600601): blowouts hand garbage-time minutes
to the bench, and actual minutes carry that outcome. The gap is concentrated in games where one
of a team's three biggest minute-takers was missing from the projection (31 test games): there
the margin RMSE is 0.843347 worse, CI [0.08003, 1.648136]. That is what an availability feed would
buy.

## Rest
`reports/m5_rest.json` regresses M5's margin residual on the home − away rest differences.
- **EuroLeague:** no feature is distinguishable from 0 in any split. For example, playing the
  previous game in the other competition gives 1.506112 points on test, 90% CI [−0.84813,
  3.860354].
- **GBL:** short rest is +5.554745 on tuning but −3.035032 on validation, so it has no stable

Rest effects in this data are too small or too noisy to forecast with, which agrees with tuning
preferring `core`.

## Flagship promotion rule (pre-registered 2026-10-09, PLAN §8.1 R1)
M5 is the EuroLeague flagship candidate; Elo stays the published forecast until this rule is met.
Declared by the owner before any look, with M5 logged from round 4 (`0.2.0+m5.d4848a7e`).
- **Games:** live games that both Elo and M5 logged before tip-off (the scorecard's provable
  rows) and that have a result, from M5's first logged round. A new M5 `model_version` restarts
  the count.
- **Statistic:** the mean per-game log-loss difference, M5 − Elo, with a paired bootstrap over
  games (10,000 resamples, fixed seed).
- **Looks:** two, and only two: after EuroLeague regular-season round 19 is complete, and after
  the season's last game. The 5% error rate is split across them (Bonferroni): each look uses a
  two-sided 97.5% CI.
- **Promote** at the first look where M5 is *clearly* better: the CI's upper bound is below 0
  **and** the mean difference is −0.01 or lower. Both are required.
- **Effect:** the site names M5 as the published forecast from the next round. Every log,
  Elo's included, keeps running (append-only). No demotion within the season. If neither look
  passes, Elo stays for 2026-27 and a rule for 2027-28 is declared before that season.
- **Command:** `eurohoops promote-check --competition euroleague --look 1|2` runs a look and
  writes `reports/promotion_euroleague_2026_look<N>.json`; it refuses until the checkpoint is complete.
  Run it with the manual workflow `promotion-look.yml`.
- **Clarifications** (declared 2026-10-11, before any look; the rule above is unchanged):
  1. Look 1 is due when round 19 exists in the regular-season schedule and every regular-season
     game of rounds up to it is played; a forfeit counts as played; a postponed earlier-round
     game keeps the look closed.
  2. Look 1 uses only regular-season games of rounds up to 19, even if later rounds are already
     played when it runs.
  3. Look 2 is due when every scheduled game of every phase in the games mart is played; it
     includes playoff games.
  4. A new flagship `model_version` restarts the count (the log is cut to its latest version);
     an Elo version change does not restart anything, its versions are listed in the verdict.
  5. Each look runs once: the command refuses to run before the look is due and refuses a
     second run once the verdict file exists.

### Reported sensitivity (added 2026-10-17, before look 1; does not change the rule)
Written into each look's report (`reports/promotion_euroleague_2026_look<N>.json`) next to the
verdict; none of it enters `promote`, and the rule above, its games, statistic, looks and CI are
unchanged.
- **Round-clustered CI** (`sensitivity_round_clustered`): the same mean gap, with whole
  season-rounds resampled with replacement instead of single games (same 10,000 resamples, seed
  and 97.5% level). Games in one round can share shocks, so this checks whether the
  game-level interval is too narrow.
- **ELOR, reported only** (`reported_only`): the roster-prior shadow model (`docs/models/elo_roster_prior.md`)
  against Elo and against M5, on the games each pair logged before tip-off, with the game-level
  CI. ELOR is a trial and is not a promotion candidate under this rule.

## Limitations
- There is no injury or lineup feed: availability is only inferred from recent box scores.
- Travel distance is not modelled (it would need a hand-made arena table).
- Player ratings are M3's and inherit its weakness on test.
- The distribution is fit in-sample on tuning (as M1), so tuning log losses are slightly
  optimistic. Validation and test are not affected.
- Live status: the EuroLeague M5 runs as a shadow log, `predictions/euroleague_m5_2026-27.csv` (`live_m5.py`, written by `eurohoops predict` only while the gate passed, from the projected roster), scored in `reports/live_scorecard.json` under `m5`, and not shown on the site. There is no live GBL M5 (not gated). It needs the 2007+ games mart and the 2011+ stints mart: the daily workflow stages them from the seeded raw cache (`ingest --from-season 2007`, `stints --mart`; a failure only skips M5), so it is logged daily in CI from 2026-10-07 (first CI run after the merge of `ci-m5-history`). Elo and M1 forecasts are identical with and without that history.

## Retune protocol v1 (declared 2026-10-11, approved by the owner)
**Declared** before any fold was scored; proposed and approved the same day. Nothing in it changes the flagship
promotion rule above, the live M5 log (`0.2.0+m5.d4848a7e`) or any report. Owner decisions of
2026-10-11 it follows: the logged flagship keeps logging unchanged and is the one R1 judges; the
retuned model logs beside it as a separate new `model_version` shadow, never shown on the site,
never counted in R1; a promotion rule for the retuned version is declared before 2027-28.
Context: `docs/models/audit_phase_d.md`.

**Baseline (what it must beat).** The current flagship exactly as logged: `proj_hc | core`,
residual half-life 180 days, ridge 40, totals M1 + rest, M3 ratings at their committed
parameters. It is one point of the grid below, so it is scored by the same code on the same folds.

**Folds: rolling-origin by season, expanding window.** Train on 2011 up to season t, score season
t+1, step one season, pool the folds. Seasons 2011 to 2014 are the ratings warm-up (as in
`backtest_m5.json`), so the scored seasons are 2015 to 2023: **nine folds** (2,236 + 331 games
at the committed splits; the owner's "about ten" becomes nine). Season labels are start years,
so 2019 is 2019-20, which is in the data and stays in. Seasons 2024-25 and 2025-26 (the used
test seasons) are in no fold, for the grid, the SE or any reported number. No random,
game-level or leave-one-out split. Everything a fold uses (ratings, projected minutes, residual,
turnover feature, margin distribution) comes from games before that game's cutoff, as in the
live path; the existing leakage tests run on the new code.

Disclosed limits of these folds. All nine seasons were already seen: 2015 to 2022 chose the
baseline and 2023 was the gate season, and M3's own parameters were chosen on them. The baseline
is therefore in-sample on these folds, which favours it; picking the best of the grid favours the
candidate. These cancel only roughly, so the folds alone never promote anything: the real test is
the shadow log.

**Grid (12 settings; every parameter listed).**
1. Residual half-life H in {120, 180, 365} days (baseline 180).
2. Minutes projection in {`proj_hc`, `proj_avail`} (baseline `proj_hc`; `proj_avail` is the
   existing declared variant that drops a player absent from the team's last 2 games).
3. Roster-turnover term T in {off, on} (baseline off). On: the carry-over of last season's team
   residual is scaled by the share of last season's minutes held by players who appear in the
   team's box scores before the cutoff (new code; this is player continuity, not the club-level
   `team_continuity.json`).

Fixed, not searched: residual ridge 40 (the baseline grid was flat between ridge 40 and 160),
M3 ratings and their parameters, rest off (no stable effect), Student-t margin model refit per
fold on earlier seasons only, no Platt step, totals M1 + rest, the draw seeds.

**Selection.** Score every setting by pooled log loss over the nine folds. The best setting has
the lowest pooled value. The standard error of a setting's gap to the best is the standard
deviation of the paired difference over 10,000 resamples of **whole seasons** (fixed seed
20261011). **One-standard-error rule:** choose the simplest setting whose pooled log loss is
within one such SE of the best. "Simplest", fixed here: fewest switched-on additions (T on
counts one, `proj_avail` counts one); then the half-life closest to 180 days; then the lower
pooled log loss. If the rule returns the baseline setting, the retune ends: no new version, the
result is reported as "the baseline stands".

**New information (the expected source of gains).** Candidates, with how each is tested without
leakage:
- Preseason roster turnover (T above): testable in the folds, from box scores before the cutoff.
  Limit: there are no historical signed-roster lists, so the feature is inferred from who has
  played, and is empty before a team's first games (it then falls back to T off).
- Availability and injury reports (`eurohoops injuries`, BasketNews snapshots): **cannot be
  backtested.** The log starts 2026-10-07 and holds one snapshot so far; there is no history.
  In the folds only the box-score proxy `proj_avail` stands in for it. The real report enters
  only forward: a later, separately declared shadow arm that uses a snapshot only if its
  `snapshot_utc` is earlier than the row's `predicted_at_utc` (itself before tip-off), stores the
  snapshot id in the row, and is judged on live games only. It is not part of this CV and cannot
  be declared before the daily snapshot job has run reliably for two weeks (owner, 2026-10-11);
  it is judged on live games only, so the count of past snapshots does not limit it further.
- Odds benchmark (`odds/`, from 2026-09-25): forward-only too. It stays a benchmark in the
  scorecard; using it as an input is out of scope here (it would also remove the benchmark).
- Rest: tested already, no stable effect (`reports/m5_rest.json`); not re-opened.

**Stopping rule.** Run once. One pass over the grid and the nine folds, on a committed commit,
with no change to the grid, the folds, the simplicity order or the criteria after the first fold
is scored. No second run, whatever the result.

**What is reported.** Pooled and per-season log loss, Brier, margin RMSE and totals CRPS for all
12 settings, nine folds each; the selected setting and the one-SE band; the paired season-level
bootstrap difference (selected minus baseline) with a 95% CI for log loss; the number of
settings within one SE; the folds' game counts. The result goes to a new report file and a
section here; no committed report is rewritten.

**Criterion to start shadow logging** (all three needed):
1. The selected setting is not the baseline setting.
2. Non-inferiority: the upper end of the 95% season-bootstrap CI of the pooled log-loss
   difference (selected minus baseline) is below +2 thousandths.
3. Worth a version: the mean difference is -2 thousandths or lower.

Superiority (the CI's upper end below 0) is reported but not required, because a shadow costs
nothing and is not shown. Passing starts a shadow with a new `model_version`, its own file
`predictions/euroleague_m5r_2026-27.csv`, logged daily from the first CI run
after the merge, with no backfill. Failing leaves the flagship alone.

**Promotion of the retuned version** is not decided here. It will be declared for 2027-28
before that season starts, on live games only, after R1 has been decided.

Owner decisions at approval (2026-10-11): grid, simplicity order and nine folds approved;
the roster-turnover code is in scope; the 2 thousandths margins stand; the injury arm waits for
two weeks of reliable daily snapshots; odds stay a benchmark, never an input; the EuroLeague M1
log keeps running through 2027-28 (retirement decided at that season's start).

### Retune protocol v1 addendum (declared 2026-10-14, before any fold is scored)

**`proj_avail` half-life.** The grid lists `proj_avail` without a decay half-life in games.
This addendum fixes it at **one game**: the best `proj_avail` point at residual half-life
`180` days and ridge `40` on the committed tuning table (`0.599292` log loss in the table
above). That choice is in-sample on those seasons; it is stated here, not re-opened in the run.

**Roster-turnover term T (when on).** At each game's round cutoff, per team, let `c` be the
share of last season's team seconds carried by players who already have a box row with seconds
greater than zero for that club in the current season before the cutoff. Let `r_full` and
`r_season` be the walk-forward team residuals from the same decayed ridge (half-life H, ridge
`40`, no rest): `r_full` on all prior rated games, `r_season` on prior rated games of the
current season only (the ridge state resets each season). Where `r_season` is not yet defined,
use `r_full`. The mixed residual is `r_T = c · r_full + (1 − c) · r_season` (per side); the
home term `h` comes from `r_full`. `c = 1` when the club has no prior-season seconds or no
current-season game before the cutoff (T off is unchanged).

**Scored set.** Rated games in fold seasons where M1, Elo and all `12` grid settings have
finite margins and win probabilities (expected `2567` = `2236` + `331` from the table above; the
run reports the actual count). Seasons after the gate test seasons are excluded from inputs
before any computation.

**Margin distribution per fold season t.** `student_t_const` refit on rated games in warm-up and
