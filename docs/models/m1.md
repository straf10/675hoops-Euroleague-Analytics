# M1: possession-based team model

Status: live shadow. M1 is logged live next to Elo.

## What it is
M1 rates every team's offence, defence and pace from box-score possessions. It turns the ratings into a predictive distribution for each game: expected points of each side, a margin distribution (P(home win) = P(margin > 0)) and a totals distribution. It is compared with a MOV-Elo tuned on the same tuning seasons.

**Live versions:**
- EuroLeague `model_version` `0.2.0+m1.cbd9aaa3`.
- Free-throw weight 0.42 (EuroLeague play-by-play); rating grid 168 points, pace grid 144 points.

**Data** (`team_games` in the marts): one row per team per rated game (played, not a forfeit), from the raw cache only.

**Seasons:**
- EuroLeague: warm-up 2007-08 to 2014-15; tuning 2015-16 to 2022-23; validation 2023-24; test 2024-25 and 2025-26.
- Fits use every earlier game.

**Excluded games:** games without box lines are still forecast and scored, but never feed a fit. In the scored seasons that is 2 tuning games and none in validation or test. The full list is the `team_games_missing` mart table.

**Possessions:** per team, `FGA - OREB + TOV + 0.42 FTA`. The game's possessions are the mean of both teams. Pace is possessions per 40 minutes. The weight is the value that makes the formula match a play-by-play possession counter on average: `w = Σ(PBP - (FGA - OREB + TOV)) / Σ FTA`. It is 0.421 on the seeded 50-game EuroLeague sample, and 0.4155 on every cached EuroLeague game from 2011-12. 0.42 is the rounded value, fixed for every season. At 0.42 the counter agrees within 2 for 0.940 of the sample's team-games and 0.902 of all team-games. The stints mart is built and validated but not used by M1.

**Equations.** Ratings are a weighted ridge on team-game rows (points per 100 possessions):

    100 * points / poss_game = mu + h * home + off[team] - def[opponent]

home = +1 for the home side's row, -1 for the away side's, 0 at a neutral venue. Row weight = `poss_game * 0.5^(age / half_life) * carry^(seasons back)`: exponential decay, plus an extra pull toward the league mean at each season start. mu and h are unpenalised. off and def carry a ridge penalty in possessions of evidence. Pace: `poss_game * 40 / minutes = mu_p + pace[home] + pace[away]`, with the same decay and a penalty in games. For home H and away A with expected pace P:

    ORtg_H = mu + h + off[H] - def[A]      ORtg_A = mu - h + off[A] - def[H]
    margin = P (ORtg_H - ORtg_A) / 100     total = P (ORtg_H + ORtg_A) / 100

Every game is forecast from a fit on games that tipped off before its round's first tip-off (walk-forward by round). The margin is `m + s * e`, with e standard Normal or Student-t(df). The scale s is constant or `s * sqrt(P / mean P)`. The total is Normal with a tuning-RMS sigma.

**Variants.** Four margin variants: Normal or Student-t, constant or pace-scaled scale. The grids and each variant's df and scale are fitted on tuning seasons only. The four are within a few thousandths of each other in log loss.

**Grids.** The v2 rating grid reaches half-life 1920 days and ridge 62.5. The pace grid reaches half-life 30 days and ridge 0.5. The EuroLeague rating half-life is 1920 days (the maximum) with carry 0.25 (the minimum): almost no decay within a span of seasons, but a strong pull to the mean at each season start. The tuning log loss is flat along these edges.

With the long v2 EuroLeague half-life, the decay no longer discounts old games; the season carry does.

**Logging:** the live M1 logs confirmed, unplayed games of every round whose first tip-off is within 48 h of the run (`predict.upcoming_games`, shared with the Elo log). A game's scored row is its latest row stamped before tip-off.

Look 1 needs round 19 complete. Look 2 needs every scheduled game of the games mart played. Promotion needs the CI upper bound below 0 and a mean difference of -0.01 or lower.

## Results
Backtest gate, validation 2023-24 (M1 minus comparison Elo, paired bootstrap 95% CI):
- EuroLeague: passed, log loss 0.5877 vs Elo 0.5885, difference -0.0008 [-0.0077, 0.0071]. Margin CRPS -0.023 [-0.085, 0.043]. Totals CRPS -1.011 [-1.393, -0.609] (Elo has no totals model; it uses the two-season mean).

Test, 2024-25 and 2025-26:
- EuroLeague: log loss 0.6232 vs Elo 0.6246, difference -0.0013 [-0.0072, 0.0052].

Calibration, validation ECE: EuroLeague 0.045 (Elo 0.057).

Tuning seasons, squared margin residual against expected pace and rating gap: EuroLeague pace slope p = 0.192, gap slope p = 0.209, pace-scaled sigma LR test p = 0.233. The heavier-tailed Student-t is what helps in the EuroLeague.

Where M1 wins and loses (validation, log loss):
- EuroLeague early rounds: 0.5947 vs Elo 0.5784. Postseason: 0.6795 vs 0.6770. Later regular season: 0.5777 vs 0.5818.
