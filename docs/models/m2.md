# M2: EuroLeague shot-quality model (xPTS)

Status: backtest only. M2 feeds no live prediction and no site page.

## What it is
M2 gives every EuroLeague field-goal attempt since 2011-12 a probability of going in. It uses where and when the shot was taken, never who took it. xPTS = P(make) × shot value.

From it come:
- team shot quality (offence and defence allowed);
- expected free-throw points (a separate team-level model);
- total shot value;
- a shrunk player shot-making measure with a stability check.

**Shots** (marts `shots`, `shots_excluded`; `docs/data/shots.md`): the cached `Points` feed, one row per field-goal attempt. 612424 shots from 2007-08 to 2025-26. 1795 are excluded for a reason: missing (0, 0) location, a 2/3 label contradicting the FIBA line by more than 0.15 m, or unparseable rows. At most 0.0053 of a validated season. Feed and box score agree on FGA and made-FG points for 0.9999 of 2011+ team-games.

**Seasons:** development 2011-12 to 2022-23 (385604 shots, leave-one-season-out CV). Validation 2023-24 (41198). Test 2024-25 and 2025-26 (93119). 2007-10 coordinates fit neither three-point line, so those shots stay in the table unused.

**Features:** distance and |angle| from the basket, `ZONE`, 2 vs 3, seconds left in the period, period (overtime as one level), score margin before the shot from the shooter's side, home, and season as a numeric trend. No shooter, team, opponent or game column (a guard and a test enforce this).

**Dropped features:** the feed's `FASTBREAK`, `SECOND_CHANCE` and `POINTS_OFF_TURNOVER` flags are not used. From 2015-16 the feed sets them only on made shots (make rate when set: 0.998 in 2015-16, 1.000 in 2022-23), so they are scoring tags, i.e. the outcome. A data audit runs at the start of every backtest: no flag or feature level may have a make rate of 99% or more (or 1% or less) on the development shots where it is set.

**Variants** (all reported):
- `spline`: logistic regression with natural cubic splines in distance for 2s and 3s separately, plus the linear features. Whitened design, fitted with numpy. Knots {4, 6, 8} × L2 {1e-5, 1e-4, 1e-3} by pooled LOSO CV log loss: 8 knots, L2 0.0001.
- `lgbm`: LightGBM, deterministic, 6 threads, no early stopping. Optuna TPE (seed 20261001, 60 trials, `n_jobs=1`) on pooled LOSO CV log loss. Best trial 57 (CV 0.629734): 15 leaves, learning rate 0.0183, 900 trees, 178 minimum child samples, feature fraction 0.955, bagging fraction 0.783, L2 0.140. The search ran on development seasons only.
- `spline_iso`, `lgbm_iso`: the same plus isotonic calibration. For development season s, the calibrator is fitted on predictions for the other development seasons, from models trained without s and that season (leave-two-out). No out-of-fold value depends on its own season.
- LightGBM predictions are the mean of five seeds. Seed sd of validation log loss: 0.000049.

**Player shot-making** (`eval/shot_making.py`): Σ(actual − xPTS) per 100 shots with out-of-fold xPTS, shrunk toward 0 by empirical Bayes. The prior variance comes from 1605 development player-seasons of at least 100 FGA (prior sd 7.71 points per 100 shots). Sampling variance is from a game-level bootstrap. 90% posterior intervals are shown for at least 100 FGA. 0.441 of the spread across players is signal (sd 11.61 raw, 5.49 shrunk).

**Team shot quality** (mart `team_shot_quality`, `reports/m2_teams.json`): per team-game and per team-season, xPTS per shot for and against, actual minus expected, expected FT points and total shot value, with game-level bootstrap intervals. Out-of-fold xPTS only.

**Season-level variant** (post-hoc): `lgbm_level` and `spline_level` add a season offset to the base model's logit. For a shot in game g, the offset mixes the season's own level so far with the previous season's full-season shift. The weight is n / (n + k) for n shots so far, with k fitted by empirical Bayes on development seasons (2312 shots for `lgbm_level`). Only shots from games that had finished by g's tip-off are used. Validation and test were seen before this variant existed, so its numbers are post-hoc, not a clean hold-out.

**Free throws:** the shot feed has no free-throw attempts on missed shots, so free throws come from play-by-play. Fouls there are untyped. A trip is tied to its shot only for and-ones (a one-shot trip after the same team's made field goal at the same clock), which carry the shot's distance band. All other trips are team-level.

| Variant | CV log loss | Validation log loss | Validation Brier | Validation ECE | Test log loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| spline | 0.631577 | 0.635097 | 0.223844 | 0.0092 | 0.635433 |
| spline_iso | 0.631513 | 0.635286 | 0.223827 | 0.0092 | 0.635587 |

| lgbm_iso | 0.629867 | 0.633333 | 0.223109 | 0.0116 | 0.633374 |

A constant league make rate scores about 0.69. Location and game state explain a modest share of make and miss.

Backtest gate (validation 2023-24): failed. Challenger `lgbm` vs baseline `spline_iso`: log loss −0.002019 (game-level bootstrap 95% CI [−0.002910, −0.001255]), Brier −0.000756 [−0.001027, −0.000509]. The chosen `lgbm` is not calibrated to the ECE target of 0.01: ECE 0.0104. Two of 20 equal-count bins miss ±0.02 (mean P 0.401: +0.0338; mean P 0.694: −0.0236).

Test (2024-25 and 2025-26): log loss −0.002119 [−0.002601, −0.001658], Brier −0.000842 [−0.001034, −0.000654]. `lgbm` ECE 0.0126.

|---|---|---|

`lgbm` is better than the spline in every band, but its calibration is worse within each band than overall. Calibration-in-the-large ratios by season range from 0.9766 to 1.0198, so league shooting level shifts between seasons. Isotonic calibration fitted on other seasons does not remove a new season's level shift (`lgbm_iso` validation ECE 0.0116).

**Free-throw share check: failed.** Per held-out season, each distance band's share of and-one FT points, and each team's share of league per-game FT points, are compared with actual values within 2 × their game-level bootstrap SE. Band flags use a count rule: at most 7 of 78 (the 95th percentile of Binomial(78, 0.0455)). Result: 12 of 78 band checks flagged. The team check fails in every season. Team mean absolute share gaps stay within their noise scale, but expected shares do not track actual ones: Pearson r runs from −0.421 (2017-18) to 0.298 (2012-13), never above its 2-SE bar. Free-throw trips and FGA compete for possessions, while the model's "other trips per FGA" assumes teams with more shots draw more fouls. A team-level FT model (trips per possession, foul drawing) is not built.

**Free-throw season level** (not gated): league FT points per team-game move between seasons (sd 0.66). The mean gap is within ±0.1 only in 2018-19, 2019-20 and 2020-21. Examples: 0.971 in 2011, −1.276 in 2021, −0.600 in 2023 (validation).

**Team comparisons, 2023-24:**

| Team | xPTS per shot | Actual − expected per shot | xPTS allowed per shot | Total shot value per game |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Panathinaikos (PAO) | 1.101 | 0.029 | 1.124 | 78.8 |
| Olympiacos (OLY) | 1.130 | -0.016 | 1.093 | 79.9 |
| Real Madrid (RMB) | 1.072 | 0.079 | 1.037 | 82.6 |

Calibration in the large (xPTS / actual FG points) is outside the 0.5% target in 7 of 12 development seasons, from 0.9766 in 2013-14 to 1.0198 in 2019-20. This is the same season-level shift. Comparisons within a season are unaffected; totals across seasons are.

**Season-level variant results** (post-hoc). `lgbm_level` does not meet the ECE target on validation (ECE 0.0100, just above 0.01; two bins near P 0.40 miss ±0.02). It does not improve log loss: level − lgbm on validation +0.000056, 95% CI [−0.000037, 0.000153]. It does fix the league level: calibration in the large over development seasons ranges from 0.9835 to 1.0131 (`lgbm`: 0.9766 to 1.0198), and 2024-25 moves from 0.9714 to 0.9934. `spline_level` does not meet the target on validation either (ECE 0.0098; the bin near P 0.40 misses ±0.02); test ECE 0.0125. Team and player numbers stay on `lgbm`.

**Player stability:** year-to-year r of shrunk shot-making over 235 player pairs with at least 200 FGA in consecutive development seasons: 0.396, 90% CI [0.311, 0.481]. Split-half (odd vs even games, 595 player-seasons): 0.315. Verdict: stable enough to show. For comparison, raw eFG% has year-to-year r 0.446 [0.343, 0.533] and raw FG% 0.672 [0.577, 0.740]. Raw percentages repeat more because they mostly repeat shot selection, which shot-making removes.

## Limitations
- The gate failed on calibration. M2 beats the spline baseline on validation and test but is not calibrated to the ECE target. It is the best available estimate of shot quality here, so read xPTS levels across seasons with care.
- Free throws: season-level variation defeats the ±0.1 reconciliation target, and the share check fails. The model has no team foul-drawing term.
- No tracking data: defender distance, shot type and shooter balance are invisible, which caps what any location model can explain.
- Player charts are sparse at 5 shots per hexagon. One season is a small sample for one player.
