# M4: GBL to EuroLeague league translation

Status: backtest only. The backtest gate failed; the factors feed M6's `proj_full` variant and the GBL list.

## What it is
Translates a player's per-100-possession box rates from one league to the other. It estimates how much a GBL season's scoring, rebounding, passing and fouling shrink or grow in the EuroLeague.

- **Inputs:** 11 rates per 100 possessions (PTS, 2PA, 3PA, FTA, OREB, DREB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV, PF). Player possessions = team possessions × minutes share, as in M3. EuroLeague 2007–2025, GBL 2018–2025. One person per player across both leagues, from the entity crosswalk.
- **Pairs** (`models/translation_pairs.py`):
  - duals: at least 300 minutes in both leagues in one season;
  - GBL→EL movers: at least 300 GBL minutes in season s, under 100 EuroLeague minutes in s, at least 300 in s+1;
  - EL→GBL movers: the reverse.
- **Model** (`models/translation.py`): per stat k, log((EL rate + c_k)/(GBL rate + c_k)) = δ_k + β_k·dual + person random effect + noise. Weighted by the harmonic mean of the two minutes. Variances by REML. δ_k has a 90% interval.
- **Team term:** `translate_team` adds the gap in the two teams' net rating of the season before.
- **Walk-forward:** for EuroLeague target season t, every fit, sd, league mean and team ratio uses only seasons before t (`eval/m4_backtest.py`).
- **Baselines:** `same_stats` (GBL rates unchanged), `team_offset` (PAO/OLY team-level EL/GBL rate ratios), `shrunk_same_stats` (shrunk toward the EuroLeague mean, prior weight 250 minutes).
- **Loss:** per mover, the mean over the 11 stats of the squared error in units of the stat's EuroLeague between-player sd. Split loss is weighted by EuroLeague minutes.
- **Limits:** no aging term and no minutes projection, so a mover's role change is part of the error. The factors come mostly from duals (PAO/OLY players in both leagues in one season), a different population from movers.

## Results
Backtest gate: failed. loss(translate) − loss(same_stats) = −0.137504, 95% CI [−0.429934, 0.018067], over 6 GBL→EL movers (2019–2023).

| Split | Movers | same_stats | team_offset | shrunk_same_stats | translate | translate_team |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tuning 2019–2022 | 5 | 0.700906 | 0.570378 | 0.612898 | 0.505893 | 0.490682 |
| Validation 2023 | 1 | 0.586152 | 0.593316 | 0.517075 | 0.699677 | 0.712428 |

Test 2024–2025: 0 GBL→EL movers.

EL→GBL movers (reported, not gated, 2019–2025): `same_stats` 0.616018, `translate` 0.613446.

Translation factors (fit for target season 2025, all pairs before 2025). δ is the log ratio EuroLeague / GBL. A GBL player keeps about 94% of his scoring rate, 84% of his assists, 87% of his steals, 90% of his free-throw attempts and 91% of his defensive rebounds, and commits about 13% more fouls. Shooting volume (2PA, 3PA), offensive rebounds and turnovers carry over unchanged within their intervals.

Team offset: the average GBL team is 20.202848 points per 100 possessions below the average EuroLeague team (95% CI [16.856517, 23.641515]), from 14 PAO/OLY club-seasons.
