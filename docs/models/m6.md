# M6: player projections and scouting

Status: published. The projections run in the daily workflow; the over/under board and "plays like" lists are built locally.

## What it is
Projects each player's per-100-possession rates (points, 3PA and FTA, assists, turnovers, offensive and defensive rebounds, steals, blocks), his TS%, 3P% and FT%, and in the EuroLeague his impact (SPM and the BRAPM posterior mean from M3). Each has an 80% interval, for next season and for the rest of a season from 25%, 50% or 75% of its rounds. Minutes are not projected; M5's `minutes.py` owns them.

Around the projections:
- an over/under board (who is shooting or impacting above what should last);
- "plays like" comparables;
- a list of young GBL players whose projected game would translate well to the EuroLeague.

**Input** (`src/eurohoops/models/player_seasons.py`): one row per person, competition and season, from regular-season box scores and the 2007–2015 Top 16 (no knockouts). Possessions are the exposure. Persons are joined across the two leagues by the entity crosswalk.

**Projection** (`src/eurohoops/models/projection.py`): per stat, the player's seasons are weighted by an exponential season decay (half-life on a grid) and by exposure. They are shrunk to the league prior of the three previous seasons by an empirical-Bayes weight (possessions for counts, attempts for percentages). The predictive variance adds the posterior variance, the season-to-season drift and the sampling noise of the target season. BRAPM snapshots enter with the noise and drift of the snapshot series (lag-1 and lag-2 moments).

**Variants** (chosen on tuning):
- `proj_shrunk`: decay and shrinkage;
- `proj_age`: adds a delta-method aging curve on regressed season-s rates, survivor corrected (`models/aging.py`);
- `proj_full`: adds M4's league translation for movers;
- `proj_full_spm`: uses the projected SPM as BRAPM's prior.

Baselines: `same_as_last`, `league_mean`, `marcel` (5/4/3 weights regressed by 1,200 possessions, with Marcel's age factor).

**Calibration:** the chosen cell's predictive sd is multiplied, per stat and checkpoint, by the square root of the mean squared standardised error of its own tuning forecasts. Means and the loss do not change.

**Loss:** per player, the squared errors of the stats standardised by a fixed between-player SD from the seasons before the first tuning target. Summed, and possession-weighted over players with at least 500 possessions in the target and a senior season before it.

**Splits:** EuroLeague tuning 2016-17 to 2022-23, validation 2023-24, test 2024-25 and 2025-26. Each target season uses only data before it (`tests/test_m6_leakage.py`).

**Gate** (validation 2023-24, point rule): the chosen variant's loss is below `marcel`'s and `same_as_last`'s, and pooled tuning plus validation 80% coverage is within 75–85% for every stat.

**Live** (`src/eurohoops/live_m6.py`, `uv run eurohoops project`): projects every player with a 2026-27 line using the chosen cell. The horizon is the largest checkpoint whose rounds are complete: next season until 25% of the rounds, so projections change four times a season. The daily workflow runs it with `--projections-only` and reads the committed crosswalk `entity/player_xwalk.csv`. The board and comparables use the newest complete season (2025-26) and do not move during the season.

**Over/under board** (`src/eurohoops/models/board.py`):
- dimensions: shot-making (points above M2's xPTS per 100 attempts), 3P% against a prior from FT%, and on/off net rating against BRAPM;
- each gap has a z score and a persistence share, `n/(n+k)` per player, from year-to-year stability on tuning seasons;
- label rule v2, with `Z_REAL` the one-sided 5% normal cut-off:
  - "too few attempts" if n < `N_MIN`. `N_MIN` is the median n of EuroLeague rotation player-seasons (at least 15 games and 15 minutes a game) over the tuning seasons, rounded down to a multiple of 10, per dimension. Player-seasons need at least one observation to count;
  - "within noise" if |z| < `Z_REAL`;
  - "likely real" if persistence × |z| ≥ `Z_REAL`;
  - "likely regression" otherwise;
- on/off has no label. Its raw value is shown with a note that it does not repeat year to year.

**"Plays like"** (`src/eurohoops/models/similarity.py`): nearest player-seasons by standardised box rates and, in the EuroLeague, shot zones and shot-making. A person never matches himself.

**GBL undervalued list:** at most 3 seasons since debut, under 20 minutes a game, and projected rates translated to the EuroLeague give an SPM in the top quarter of GBL rotation players.

## Limitations
- Minutes are not projected. Injuries and roster changes are not known to the model.
- Newcomers without a usable season get the league prior and the `no_history` flag, so their intervals are wide.
- Players without a known age take the no-aging path. The chosen variant does not age anyone.
- BRAPM is EuroLeague-only and starts in 2011-12. The GBL list's SPM is the EuroLeague SPM on translated rates, not a GBL impact measure.
- M4's translation factors did not pass their gate. They enter only the GBL list and `proj_full`.
