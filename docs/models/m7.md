# M7: season simulator

Status: published for the EuroLeague (Standings page season simulation, daily log). GBL output is labelled "not gated".

## What it is
M7 simulates the rest of a regular season many times and turns the simulated final tables into probabilities: each team's final-place distribution, P(direct playoffs), P(play-in), P(top 10), and through the knockouts P(Final Four) and P(title). Each simulation first draws every team's strength from M1's posterior, so uncertainty about how good a team is spreads the outcomes, not only game-to-game noise. The strength is kept for the whole season. Every remaining fixture is played with M1's margin model. The table is ranked with the official tie-breaks, then the play-in, playoffs and Final Four are played (GBL: quarterfinals, semifinals, final).

**Strength posterior** (`models/team_eff.py`, `rating_posteriors`): M1's ridge on team-game offensive ratings is read as a Gaussian posterior over [μ, h, off, def]. The mean is the ridge solution at the cutoff. The covariance is σ² · (G + P)⁻¹, with G the decayed, possession-weighted Gram matrix, P the ridge penalty, and σ² the ridge residual variance with the effective degrees of freedom subtracted. On 200 synthetic leagues its 90% intervals cover the truth within 87–93%. Pace is M1's pace fit at the cutoff, a point estimate. M1's committed parameters are used.

**Games** (`sim/season.py`): margin = pace · (ORtg_H − ORtg_A) / 100 + noise. Noise is M1's Student-t (EuroLeague df 7), rounded. A 0 becomes ±1, because overtime is not modelled and overtime points count for no tie-break. Scores follow from the expected total, so score tie-breaks have scores.

**Tables** (`standings.rank`, plus `rank_by_wins` for speed): the EuroLeague Art. 19 procedure with deductions. It reproduces the official final table of all 8 scored EuroLeague seasons from the games mart (overtime games on their regulation score).

**Formats** (`sim/formats.py`):

| seasons | teams | rounds | direct playoffs | play-in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EuroLeague 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19 | 16 | 30 | 1–8 | — |
| EuroLeague 2020-21, 2022-23 | 18 | 34 | 1–8 | — |
| EuroLeague 2023-24, 2024-25 | 18 | 34 | 1–6 | 7–10 |
| EuroLeague 2025-26, 2026-27 | 20 | 38 | 1–6 | 7–10 |
| GBL 2020-21, 2022-23 | 12 | 22 | 1–8 | — |
| GBL 2021-22, 2025-26 | 13 | 26 | 1–8 | — |
| GBL 2026-27 | 14 | 26 | 1–8 | — |

Excluded: EuroLeague 2015-16 (groups and Top 16), 2019-20 (cancelled), 2021-22 (Russian clubs removed mid-season); GBL 2023-24 and 2024-25 (the quarterfinal field is not the table's top 8, cause not in any source held). Bylaws exist online only for 2025-26 and 2026-27; earlier cut lines come from the official final tables and the playoff fields in the EuroLeague games feed (`scripts/checks/m7_facts.py`).

**Evaluation** (`eval/m7_backtest.py`):
- Checkpoints after 25%, 50% and 75% of a season's rounds (cutoff = the next round's first tip-off). Everything a checkpoint uses comes from earlier games (`tests/test_m7_leakage.py`). 4,000 simulations per checkpoint, seed 20261101 + checkpoint index.
- Targets: the real final table's direct-playoff cut (top 8 through 2022-23, top 6 after), the top 10 from 2023-24, and the final place.
- Variants, chosen on tuning by lowest RPS of the final place: `sim_full` (posterior draws, M1's full noise), `sim_net` (noise shrunk so strength + noise variance equals M1's predictive variance), and `sim_inflate` (sim_net with the covariance × 1.5 or 2).
- Baselines: `point_sim` (M1 point strengths, no strength uncertainty), `elo_sim` (comparison-Elo strengths), `standings_now` (the current table as the final one).

**Gate v2** (`eval/m7_gate_v2.py`, `backtest_m7_v2.json`): rolling origin with an expanding window. For each scored EuroLeague season (2017-18, 2018-19, 2020-21, 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26; 372 team-checkpoints), the variant is chosen by the lowest pooled final-place RPS on earlier eligible seasons. Pass requires all three:
1. the pooled direct-cut Brier of the chosen variant minus `point_sim` has a season-cluster bootstrap 95% CI with upper bound below +0.005;
2. pooled Brier below `standings_now`'s;
3. pooled Spiegelhalter |z| < 1.96.

M7 passed gate v2, so `simulate` runs in the daily workflow (`.github/workflows/daily.yml`). The Standings page shows no gate label (removed in 339bc71).

Backtest gate v1 (validation 2023-24): failed. `sim_full` beats `standings_now` but not `point_sim`: Brier difference +0.003675, team-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [−0.003868, 0.010623]. Pooled z −0.870358 is inside 1.96, so the failure is the point rule, not calibration. `point_sim` is ahead at each validation checkpoint (25% checkpoint: `sim_full` 0.127834, `point_sim` 0.122470; 75%: 0.046499 and 0.045464). On test the order flips: `sim_full` − `point_sim` = −0.000939 (CI [−0.007556, 0.004971]). Test: `sim_full` − `standings_now` = −0.094603 (CI [−0.170126, −0.033202]). The simulator is calibrated (|z| < 1.96 in every EuroLeague split).

**Gate v2: passed** (`uv run eurohoops backtest --model m7 --gate-v2`; 372 team-checkpoints over 7 seasons). The rolling origin chose `sim_full` at every origin, so the verdict is v1's variant. Every scored season was already seen by v1, so this is a larger-sample re-evaluation, not a clean hold-out.
- (a) Non-inferiority: `sim_full` − `point_sim` Brier −0.000139, season-cluster bootstrap 95% CI [−0.003839, 0.003782] (7 whole seasons, 10,000 resamples). Upper bound is below the +0.005 margin.
- (b) Pooled Brier 0.111728, below `standings_now`'s 0.182796 (difference −0.071068, CI [−0.090278, −0.048401]).
- (c) Pooled Spiegelhalter z −0.679161, |z| < 1.96.

`point_sim`'s pooled Brier is 0.111867. Drawing strengths from M1's posterior neither helps nor hurts measurably.

**Reliability** (test, `sim_full`): predicted shares follow observed shares bin by bin (`backtest_m7.json`, `reliability.test.sim_full`). 44 team-checkpoints fall in the 0–0.1 bin, and none was made.

**Title odds** (reported, not gated): mean Brier over teams' P(title), summed per checkpoint. `sim_full` 1.126624 on validation and 0.887707 on test (`elo_sim` 0.866727 on test).

**GBL** (reported; EuroLeague choice applied as fixed; tuning 2020-21 to 2022-23, test 2025-26): on tuning, `sim_full` has Brier 0.103094 against `point_sim`'s 0.108201. `point_sim` is miscalibrated (z 2.771) and `sim_full` is not (z 0.924). On the one test season (13 teams), every model is miscalibrated (`sim_full` z 3.016). GBL rules are unverified (playoff places, series lengths, tie-breaks) and are carried into every GBL output.

## Live
`uv run eurohoops simulate [--competition gbl] [--dry-run]` (`src/eurohoops/live_sim.py`) simulates the 2026-27 regular season after its completed rounds (10,000 simulations, `sim_full`). It appends one row per team to `predictions/{competition}_sim_2026-27.csv`, once per completed round. The EuroLeague runs in the daily workflow. `simulate --competition gbl` never appends; the GBL Standings table is labelled "not gated".

## Limitations
- Strengths are fixed within a simulation: no form, injuries or roster changes after the cutoff.
- Overtime is not modelled; a tied regulation margin is a coin flip.
- M1's decay is a fractional likelihood, so the posterior describes the decayed average strength. The inflation variants that would widen it did worse on tuning.
- A team with no game yet (a promoted GBL club before round 1) starts at the average strength of the teams that left the league, with M1's wide prior variance. Its odds stay wide until it plays.
- GBL formats and tie-breaks are unverified. Two GBL seasons are excluded.
- Small samples: gate v1 rests on one validation season (18 teams × 3 checkpoints).
- Gate v2 has one input defect: the 2016-17 selection season was simulated without regulation scores of its overtime games. The defect touches only that season's rows and the pooled origin RPS table. Replacing those rows with correct values leaves `sim_full` chosen at every origin, and the three criteria are unchanged.
